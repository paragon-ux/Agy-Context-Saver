# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, design, or mistakes.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to technical disagreement.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on the work rather than the person.
- Share only information you are authorized to disclose. Do not post private transcripts, secrets, credentials, or personal configuration data.
- Make space for different experience levels and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable. Threats or attempts to exploit project users are also not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations, reviewing reports of unacceptable behavior, and taking proportionate and fair corrective action. They may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or ban contributors for conduct they determine to be abusive or harmful.

## Scope

This policy applies within project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, release spaces, and other project-related public or private communication channels.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private reporting tools or the contact channel listed by the repository owner. Do not include exploit details or another person's sensitive data in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports with discretion and share them only with people who need to review or resolve the issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports, ask for additional information where needed, and decide on an appropriate response based on the facts and context. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply promptly. A maintainer's decision may be appealed privately to the repository owner or another maintainer where one is available.

This code of conduct is intentionally short and project-specific. Maintainers may replace it with a recognized community standard, such as the Contributor Covenant, if they adopt that standard consistently across project spaces.
